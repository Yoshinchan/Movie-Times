Toxic

Action • Crime • Romance • Thriller


Some stories aren't meant for children. Set against a dangerous world of power, crime, ambition, and betrayal, Toxic follows a mysterious man whose arrival threatens to shake an entire underworld empire. As hidden alliances begin to collapse and old enemies emerge, he is pulled into a violent battle where trust is rare and power comes at a price. Beneath the glamour and chaos lies a darker story of family, loyalty, revenge, and survival. With a ruthless underworld, unforgettable characters, and a larger-than-life protagonist at its center, Toxic promises a stylish and intense journey into a world where nobody is truly innocent. Enter the world of TOXIC. Where power is addictive. And every fairy tale has a dark side.
