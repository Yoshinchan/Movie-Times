## THE DARK KNIGHT **Why so serious?** When Gotham City is threatened by a mysterious criminal mastermind known as the Joker, Batman faces an enemy unlike anyone he has encountered before. As chaos spreads through the city, Bruce Wayne is forced to confront the limits of his own beliefs and the true cost of being Gotham's protector. The Joker's unpredictable games push Batman, the police, and the entire city toward a battle between order and chaos. **One fights for justice. One lives for chaos. And Gotham will never be the same.** **THE DARK KNIGHT — Sometimes, the line between hero and villain is darker than you think.**
