Krishnavataram

Action • Lean • Romance • God


A story older than time itself. From the enchanting world of Vrindavan to the majestic kingdom of Dwarka, Krishnavataram follows Lord Krishna through a journey of love, devotion, duty, sacrifice, and destiny. Told through the eyes of Satyabhama, the film explores the many dimensions of Krishna — the playful soul of Vrindavan, the eternal beloved of Radha, the wise philosopher, the compassionate protector, and the powerful king of Dwarka. As love, duty, and destiny pull him in different directions, Krishna's journey becomes more than a story of a divine figure — it becomes a timeless reflection on life, relationships, sacrifice, and the meaning of true love. He came to love. He lived for dharma. And he changed the course of history. KRISHNAVATARAM — Where love becomes divine, and destiny becomes eternal
