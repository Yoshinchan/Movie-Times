## OPPENHEIMER **The world forever changed in a single moment.** J. Robert Oppenheimer, a brilliant physicist, is chosen to lead a secret wartime project that brings together some of the greatest scientific minds of the era. Their mission is extraordinary: develop a weapon powerful enough to reshape the course of history. As the project moves closer to its terrifying success, Oppenheimer finds himself caught between scientific ambition, political pressure, personal relationships, and the consequences of creating something humanity has never witnessed before. What begins as a race against time becomes a profound struggle with power, responsibility, and the price of knowledge. **He wanted to understand the universe. He helped create something that could destroy it.** **OPPENHEIMER — The man who changed the world forever.**
