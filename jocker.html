Arthur Fleck is a lonely man living on the edge of society, struggling to find purpose while working as a clown and aspiring stand-up comedian in a city consumed by fear, inequality, and chaos. As rejection, isolation, and a series of painful encounters push him further away from the world around him, Arthur slowly transforms into a darker version of himself. What begins as a desperate search for acceptance becomes a disturbing journey into madness, identity, and rebellion. In a city that refuses to see him, Arthur decides to make the world watch.
