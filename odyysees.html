## THE ODYSSEY **A legendary journey. A battle against the impossible.** After the fall of Troy, Odysseus begins an epic journey home to Ithaca. But the road back is filled with mythical dangers, powerful gods, monstrous creatures, and trials that test his courage, intelligence, and determination. Separated from his kingdom and the people he loves, Odysseus must survive an extraordinary voyage across an ancient world where every victory comes at a price. As years pass and his journey becomes a fight against both the forces around him and the struggles within himself, one question remains: **Can a hero who has faced the gods themselves finally find his way home?** **THE ODYSSEY — The greatest journey ever told.**
